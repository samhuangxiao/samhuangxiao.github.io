Xiao (Sam) Huang

I’m a fourth-year Computer Science student at UC Berkeley, advised by Professors David Wagner and Raluca Ada Popa.

My research focuses on AI Safety and Security, specifically exploring vulnerabilities in AI models and systems, responsibly deploying AI, and red-teaming emerging technologies.

Publications
  • MiniScope: A Least Privilege Framework for Authorizing Tool Calling Agents
    Jinhao Zhu, Kevin Tseng, Gil Vernik, Xiao Huang, Shishir G. Patil, Vivian Fang, Raluca Ada Popa
    Under Review

    We introduce a framework to enforce least-privilege principles for LLM Agents. MiniScope intercepts tool calls to verify user intent against allowable actions, effectively mitigating privilege escalation and unauthorized tool execution in agentic systems.

  • GradShield: Alignment Preserving Finetuning
    Zhanhao Hu*, Xiao Huang*, Patrick Mendoza, Emad A. Alghamdi, Raluca Ada Popa, David Wagner
    ICLR 2026

    A defense mechanism designed to protect safety alignment during the fine-tuning process. We propose a gradient-direction filtering algorithm that detects and removes poisonous data samples, preventing the model from learning harmful behaviors from compromised datasets.

  • JailbreaksOverTime: Detecting Jailbreak Attacks Under Distribution Shift
    Julien Piet, Xiao Huang, Dennis Jacob, Annabella Chow, Maha Alrashed, Geng Zhao, Zhanhao Hu, Chawin Sitawarin, Basel Alomair, David Wagner
    AISEC 2025

    We curated a large-scale dataset of jailbreak attacks containing temporal metadata to benchmark LLM defenses. Our analysis reveals how attack patterns shift over time and highlights the necessity for continuous learning solutions to detect evolving threats.
